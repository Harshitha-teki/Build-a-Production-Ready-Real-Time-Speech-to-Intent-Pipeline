# Vantage Auto Loan

The Vantage Auto Loan offers borrow rates starting at 5.49 percent annual percentage rate for new vehicles.
Vantage Auto Loan terms range from 36 to 72 months depending on the vehicle age.
Used vehicles older than seven model-years do not qualify for Vantage Auto Loan financing.
A down payment of at least 10 percent is recommended for the Vantage Auto Loan.
Customers can pay off the Vantage Auto Loan early with no prepayment penalty.
The loan requires comprehensive and collision insurance for the full loan term.
Rate discounts of 0.25 percent apply when the loan payment is auto-deducted from a Summit Checking Account.
Final approval is subject to vehicle appraisal and debt-to-income ratio review.

# Account Closure Policy

The Account Closure Policy requires a 30-day notice period before an account is closed.
Accounts with an outstanding negative balance cannot be closed until the balance is settled.
Closure of a Summit Checking Account within 90 days of opening incurs a $25 early account closure fee.
Customers who close an account must redeem or transfer any points accrued under the Cloud Rewards Program.
Recurring payments linked to a closed account are automatically cancelled by the bank.
The final statement is made available for download for 24 months after closure.
Reopening an account after closure requires a new application and credit check.
