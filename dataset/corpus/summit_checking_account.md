# Summit Checking Account

The Summit Checking Account charges a monthly maintenance fee of $12.00.
Customers can waive the monthly fee by maintaining a minimum daily balance of $1,500.
Performing a direct deposit of at least $500 per month also waives the maintenance fee.
The account comes with free access to more than 55,000 fee-free ATMs nationwide.
Out-of-network ATM withdrawals incur a $2.50 surcharge per transaction.
There is no minimum opening deposit required for the Summit Checking Account.
Account holders receive a free debit card with chip and contactless payment support.
The account supports mobile check deposit with next-business-day funds availability.
Overdraft coverage can be added at no additional charge when linked to a Horizon Savings Account.

# Overdraft Protection Policy

Overdraft Protection automatically transfers available funds from a linked savings account to cover a debit.
Enrollment in Overdraft Protection is available to all Summit Checking Account holders at no cost.
Each automatic transfer triggered by Overdraft Protection costs a flat fee of $5.00.
Transfers are limited to a maximum of six per calendar day.
If the linked savings account also lacks sufficient funds, the transfer is declined and an overdraft occurs.
Covered debit transactions are processed up to a balance of negative $250 with standard overdraft coverage.
A standard non-covered overdraft incurs a fee of $34.00 per occurrence.
Customers can manage Overdraft Protection enrollment through the mobile app.
