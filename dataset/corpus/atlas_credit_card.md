# Atlas Credit Card

The Atlas Credit Card earns 2 percent cash back on every purchase with no category limits.
New cardholders receive a $200 statement credit after spending $1,000 in the first three months.
The Atlas Credit Card offers an introductory annual percentage rate of 0 percent for the first 12 months.
After the introductory period, the variable annual percentage rate ranges from 18.99 to 26.99 percent.
There is no annual fee for the Atlas Credit Card.
Foreign transactions made with the Atlas Credit Card incur a 2.5 percent currency conversion fee.
Monthly statements are generated on the first day of each month and due by the 25th.
Cardholders can set spending alerts and monthly budget limits inside the mobile app.

# Foreign Transactions Policy

The Foreign Transactions Policy applies a 2.5 percent fee to purchases made in a foreign currency.
Purchases in U.S. dollars made outside the country do not incur a currency conversion fee.
The currency conversion rate used is the market rate published at the time of the transaction.
ATM withdrawals abroad also fall under the Foreign Transactions Policy.
There is no separate fee for declined foreign transactions.
Visa and Mastercard network processing fees are absorbed by the bank and never passed to the cardholder.
Transactions from sanctioned or restricted countries are blocked automatically by the bank.
