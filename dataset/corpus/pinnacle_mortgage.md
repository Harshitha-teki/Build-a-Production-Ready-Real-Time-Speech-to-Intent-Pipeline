# Pinnacle Mortgage

The Pinnacle Mortgage offers a fixed-rate 30-year term starting at 6.25 percent annual percentage rate.
A 20 percent down payment avoids private mortgage insurance on the Pinnacle Mortgage.
The minimum down payment for a Pinnacle Mortgage is 5 percent for qualified first-time homebuyers.
Private mortgage insurance costs roughly 0.5 percent of the loan amount per year.
Closing costs for the Pinnacle Mortgage average between 2 and 5 percent of the purchase price.
Prepaying the Pinnacle Mortgage in full carries no prepayment penalty.
Escrow accounts for property taxes and insurance are required when the down payment is below 20 percent.
Loan payments are due on the first of each month with a 15-day grace period.

# Credit Limit Increase Policy

The Credit Limit Increase Policy automatically reviews eligible cardholder accounts every six months.
Cardholders may also request a manual credit limit increase at any time through the mobile app.
Manual credit limit increase requests require a current annual income verification.
Approving a credit limit increase triggers a hard credit inquiry on the applicant's report.
Customers must have the account open for at least six months before requesting an increase.
Automatic increases under the Credit Limit Increase Policy do not trigger a hard inquiry.
Credit limit increase denials can be appealed within 30 days with updated income documents.
