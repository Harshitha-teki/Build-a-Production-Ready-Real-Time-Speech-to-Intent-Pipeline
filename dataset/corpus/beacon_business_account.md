# Beacon Business Account

The Beacon Business Account charges a monthly service fee of $15.00 for accounts with no minimum balance.
The monthly service fee is waived when the account maintains a $5,000 average daily balance.
The Beacon Business Account includes free incoming wire transfers within the United States.
The account bundles free invoice creation and payment tracking for up to 25 invoices per month.
Up to five named users can be granted card and account access with individual spending limits.
Unlimited digital receipts are included with the Beacon Business Account.
Cash deposits over $10,000 in a single day require a completed currency transaction report.
The Beacon Business Account provides same-day ACH payment processing when initiated before 2 p.m.

# Fraud Liability Policy

The Fraud Liability Policy guarantees zero liability for unauthorized transactions reported within 60 days.
Customers must report unauthorized activity to the bank within 60 days to remain protected.
Reported fraudulent transactions are temporarily credited back within two business days.
Temporarily credited funds remain subject to final review of the investigation outcome.
A fraud investigation can take up to 10 business days to complete.
Customers who share credentials or authorize a third party lose zero-liability protection under the Fraud Liability Policy.
Card-linked mobile wallets are covered by the same zero-liability guarantee.
Suspicious transactions detected by the bank are blocked and verified with the customer before release.
