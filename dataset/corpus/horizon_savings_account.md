# Horizon Savings Account

The Horizon Savings Account offers an annual percentage yield of 3.25 percent on balances up to $25,000.
Balances above $25,000 earn a reduced annual percentage yield of 2.00 percent.
There is a minimum balance of $100 required to open the Horizon Savings Account.
Savings accounts allow up to six free withdrawals per statement cycle.
Each withdrawal beyond the sixth free withdrawal costs $10.00.
Interest is compounded daily and credited to the account at the end of each month.
The Horizon Savings Account has no monthly maintenance fee.
Customers can automatically schedule recurring transfers from a linked Summit Checking Account.

# Interest Rate Tiering Policy

Interest Rate Tiering Policy applies higher rates to higher average daily balances.
Under the Interest Rate Tiering Policy, the first $25,000 earns the full advertised rate.
Tier two covers balances from $25,000 to $100,000 at a rate 0.75 percentage points lower.
Tier three covers balances above $100,000 at a rate 1.25 percentage points lower than tier one.
The tier applied each month uses the average daily balance for that statement cycle.
Qualifying direct deposits can bump the customer into a higher tier for one cycle.
Rates under the Interest Rate Tiering Policy are reviewed quarterly and may be adjusted.
