# Shield Home Insurance

The Shield Home Insurance policy covers dwelling, personal property, and liability at replacement cost.
The standard deductible for Shield Home Insurance is $1,000.
Choosing a $2,500 deductible lowers the annual premium by 12 percent.
Shield Home Insurance covers water damage from burst pipes but excludes flood and earthquake damage.
Personal property coverage equals 70 percent of the dwelling coverage amount.
The policy includes $500,000 of personal liability coverage per occurrence.
Claims are typically settled within 14 days of the adjuster's approval.
Shield Home Insurance discounts of 10 percent apply when home and auto policies are bundled.

# Mobile App Security Settings

The mobile app requires two-factor authentication for every new device sign-in.
Biometric authentication such as face or fingerprint can replace the account password after setup.
Automatic session timeout locks the app after five minutes of inactivity.
Customers can remotely revoke access to a lost or stolen device from the security settings menu.
The app notifies the customer instantly when a new device attempts to sign in.
Sensitive actions such as transferring over $2,000 require a re-authentication step.
Security alerts can be delivered by push notification, SMS, or email.
